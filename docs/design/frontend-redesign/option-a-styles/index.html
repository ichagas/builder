Option A: three visual styles

The structure, flows and code behind these three pages are identical (shared/app.js + shared/base.css). Only the visual layer differs. Every frame is live. The scenario is the Benefits Intake Portal after its first release: reported bugs, requested enhancements, a feature in design, and the next release. See EVOLVE.md and, for the building-vs-released comparison, compare-lifecycle.html.

Show

1. Clarity

Open full screen

A calm, light public-service UI. Generous spacing, soft elevation and one confident blue. Phase colors appear only in the rail and page markers. Public Sans throughout. Best if Pronghorn must feel trustworthy to non-technical ministry staff.

Desktop 1440 × 900
Phone 390 × 800

2. Blueprint

Open full screen

An engineering drawing. Navy chrome, a faint drafting grid, near-square corners, denser rows, and IBM Plex with the mono face kept for identifiers (WI-42, branches, versions). Best for delivery teams who spend all day in it and want more on screen.

Desktop 1440 × 900
Phone 390 × 800

3. Studio

Open full screen

Dark, soft and expressive, built for long focused sessions. Each phase owns a color, and the page takes on the color of wherever you are: the background glow, heading, active tab and highlighted canvas nodes. Rounded shapes and pill buttons. Onest. Best if Pronghorn wants a distinctive product identity.

Desktop 1440 × 900
Phone 390 × 800
